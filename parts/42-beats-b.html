Beat 2

Boom-tick loop (your first recorded beat)

The famous beat, recorded into the SEQ. The trick: steps of 1/8 and a loop of 8 steps, so every step gets a hit and there are no gaps to skip.

  • Load MY DRUM, press Save, press Oct− once. ARP off.
  • Press SEQ. Page 2: Voice = MY DRUM's number · Rate = 1/8 · Tempo = 100 · Gate = 30%.
  • Page 1: Pattern = 1 · Step = 8. If it has old notes, Clear it (Knob 2, then Knob 4).
  1. Press Rec.
  2. Press these bottom keys one at a time: 1, 16, 9, 16, 1, 16, 9, 16.
  3. Press Rec again. "boom – tick – SNAP – tick," forever
  4. Press Save. Write "Pattern 1 = Beat 2" in your log.

The bottom row says which key to press for each step.

  • Double boom: 1, 16, 9, 16, 1, 1, 9, 16.
  • Dance: 1, 16, 1, 16, 1, 16, 1, 16 at Tempo 124.
  • Bounce: while it plays, page 3, Swing about 60%.   New kit: page 2, Voice = 123 TOM TOMS.
Beat 3

Lazy bounce (with gaps)

Real beats breathe. This one uses all 16 steps with silent gaps between hits: a slow, head-nodding feel.

  • MY DRUM, saved. Oct− once. Press SEQ.
  • Page 2: Voice = MY DRUM · Rate = 1/16 · Tempo = 88 · Gate = 40%.
  • Page 1: Pattern = 2 · Step = 16.   Page 3: Swing = 60%.

"gap" = hold Rec and turn Select one click.

  1. Press Rec. Say each step out loud as you go. It helps.
  2. Follow the bottom row: 1 · gap · 16 · gap · 9 · gap · 16 · 1 · gap · 1 · 16 · gap · 9 · gap · 16 · gap.
  3. Press Rec to listen. Wrong note? Lesson 7b. Or Clear it and go again. Everyone does.
  4. Press Save.
Why it grooves

The BOOM on step 8 lands just before the middle of the bar. That early kick, plus swing, is the lazy bounce. Try Tempo 70 and Swing 66% for extra lazy.

Beat 4

Bass line, then a song

A bass line is the low, bouncy tune under a beat. This one uses 5 keys. Then we chain it to your drums.

  • Turn Presets to 035 BASS 1. Press Save. Press Oct− once.
  • Press SEQ. Page 2: Voice = 035 · Rate = 1/8 · Tempo = 100 · Gate = 60%.
  • Page 1: Pattern = 3 · Step = 16.
  1. Press Rec. Follow the bottom row: 3 · gap · 3 · 10 · 3 · gap · 5 · 6 · 3 · gap · 3 · 10 · 2 · gap · 5 · 6. Key 3 is "home"; key 10 is the same note one floor up.
  2. Press Rec to listen, then Save.

Make it a song: chain the patterns

Chain plays patterns one after another, each with its own sound. Drums, then bass, then drums: a little song.

  1. Give patterns 1, 2 and 3 (Beats 2, 3 and 4) the same Tempo.
  2. Page 1: Pattern = 1, Knob 3 Chain = 3. Press Play/Stop. 1, then 2, then 3, then round again
  3. Press Save.

The sounds take turns: the FM-1 can't play drums and bass at the same time. That's the box, not you.
