Lesson 8

Save and undo

Changes you make with ENV, LFO, FX or Edit are only kept if you save. Here's how to keep them, throw them away, or start over.

Keep it

  1. Press Save and hold it a moment. Preset Saved. Your sound, effects, patterns and settings are all kept.
It writes over the preset you're on

Save while on 065 BRASS 1 and the old BRASS 1 is replaced by your version. You can always get the factory one back (below). Write down what you saved in the log on page .

Throw away changes you haven't saved

  1. Turn Presets one click away and one click back. the saved version comes back

Put the factory sounds back

  1. Press GLO. Turn Select to page 2.
  2. Turn Knob 1 (Reset Patches). it asks which group: A (001–032), B (033–064), C (065–096) or D (097–128)
  3. Pick the group with Knob 1–Knob 4, then turn Select right to reset, or left to cancel. Press GLO to close.

Extras in GLO page 2: Knob 2 Velocity = how hard every key "hits" (the keys aren't touch-sensitive). Knob 4 Glide = notes slide into each other; it only works in MONO (Lesson 9).

Lesson 9

Build your own sound

Remember the 6 swing kids? Some are singers you hear, some are pushers that change a singer's tone. This lesson lets you change who pushes whom, and how hard.

Swap the team: Algorithm

  1. Pick a sound and hold a note (or use the ARP with Latch on, so both hands are free).
  2. Turn Algorithm one click at a time. a picture of the 6 kids flashes up, and the sound changes each click
  3. Like one? Press Save. Don't? Undo with Presets (Lesson 8).

Meet each kid: Edit

  1. Press Edit. the top keys start blinking. They are now buttons: OP1–OP6, PIT, GLO, MONO, POLY
  2. Press the top key labeled OP1. Turn Select to page 4.
  3. Turn Knob 4 (Out Level) to lowest, then back up. that kid disappears, then returns. Try OP2 to OP6 the same way
  4. On page 3, Knob 2 (FreqCoarse) changes a kid's "size": warm to bell-like.
  5. Press Edit to leave. Press Save if you like it.
Two more tricks

MONO / POLY: in Edit, the MONO key makes it play one note at a time (great for bass). POLY = up to 12 at once.
Fizz: in Edit, press the top key GLO and turn up Feedback (Knob 2). At 7 it hisses like a cymbal.

Lesson 10

Plug things in

The back edge has a headphone jack, a MIDI IN jack, a USB-C port and the power switch. Here's what to plug into what.

I want to…Do this
Hear it privatelyWired headphones in the 3.5 mm jack. The speaker turns off by itself.
Hear it louderAn aux cable (3.5 mm both ends) from the jack to a speaker's AUX IN. Start with Master low.
Charge itAny USB-C charger or computer port. It plays while charging.
Play music apps with its keysA USB-C cable to a computer. Apps see the FM-1 as a keyboard.
Add thousands of soundsUSB-C to a computer plus the free program Dexed, which can send classic DX7 sound sets to the FM-1.
Play it from another keyboardThe MIDI IN jack, with a "TRS Type A" adapter. Most people never need this.
Buzz or hiss?

Use headphones with a 2-ring plug, turn Master down, and turn off Distortion in FX.
