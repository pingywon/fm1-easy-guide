First try: press Home. Then find your problem below.

Disable SEQ to enterPress SEQ to turn it off, then ARP.

What's happeningWhy, and the fix
My sound changed when I pressed SEQEach pattern has its own sound. Save yours, then pick it on SEQ page 2 (Lesson 7).
Presets knob does nothingYou're in SEQ. Pick the sound on page 2, or press SEQ to leave.
Keys blink and don't playYou're in Edit. Press Edit to leave.
It keeps playing by itselfARP Latch (press ARP) or the SEQ (press Play/Stop).
Only one note at a timeMONO is on. Edit → top key POLY → Edit.
Muffled or very quietThe Filter in FX. Turn it off with Knob 4.
Wobbly or pulsingThe LFO. Set PMD and AMD to 0.
Crackles on chordsIt's loud. Turn Master down.
Bluetooth headphones won't connectThey can't. It's not that kind of Bluetooth. Use the jack.
Still stuck? The 4-step rescue: (1) Home, and turn off ARP or SEQ if lit. (2) Oct− + Oct+ together. (3) Presets one click away and back. (4) Power off and on.
