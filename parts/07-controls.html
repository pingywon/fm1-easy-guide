Part 1

Every control in one line

Numbers match the map on page . The lesson number tells you where it's taught.

1 MasterVolume. The only knob with an end-stop.
2 SelectTurns the page inside a drawer. In FX, picks which effect.
3 PresetsPicks one of the 128 sounds. Lesson 1.
4 AlgorithmChanges how the sound is built (32 choices). Lesson 9.
5 Oct− Oct+Whole keyboard lower or higher. Both together = reset. Lesson 2.
6 ScreenTop line: your sound. Bottom line: the drawer and its page.
7 Knob 1–4Different jobs in every drawer. Read the screen.
8 FX6 effects: reverb, echo, crunch… Lesson 5.
9 SelInside FX only: grab an effect to move it in the line.
10 ENVHow a sound starts, holds and fades. Lesson 3.
11 LFOMakes the sound wobble or pulse. Lesson 4.
12 EditBuild sounds from scratch. The keys become buttons. Lesson 9.
13 GLOMachine settings: factory reset, key strength, glide. Lesson 8.
14 HomeCloses any drawer. Shows the wiggly line.
15 SaveKeeps everything. Hold it a moment. Lesson 8.
16 ARPPlays held keys for you, one after another. Lesson 6.
17 SEQThe 16-step music box. Lesson 7.
18 Play/StopStarts and stops the music box.
19 RecRecords into the music box.
20 Keys16 bottom, 11 top. Up to 12 notes at once.
21–24 Back edgeHeadphone jack · MIDI IN · USB-C · power switch. Lesson 10.
