Part 3 · Beats

Can two sounds play together?

Short answer on the stock software: no. (On the free SLOOP firmware: yes — page .) The FM-1 speaks with one voice at a time. It can play up to 12 notes of that one sound together, but drums and bass can never sound at the same moment. That's the box, not you — and the manual never says it out loud.

And no, there's no mixer

  • Master is one volume for the whole box — everything louder or quieter together.
  • The operator levels inside Edit blend the six ingredients of one single sound, not different instruments.
  • The Mix knobs in FX blend "plain sound vs. echo," not instrument vs. instrument.

What you CAN do

1 · Put several notes on one step — they play together.

  1. In SEQ, press Rec.
  2. Press two or three keys at the same time and let go together. that whole handful lands on ONE step
  3. Play it back: the notes sound as one hit. Keys 1 + 16 together = BOOM and tick at once.
  • 2 · Make one sound do two jobs. The MY DRUM trick (page ): the same sound is a thump on the low keys and a tick up high. One voice, but your ear hears a kit.
  • 3 · Take turns with Chain (page ): drums for a bar, then bass for a bar. Sounds like a song, never overlaps.
  • 4 · To truly layer, use a recorder. Aux cable from the FM-1 into any phone or computer recorder. Record loop #1 playing, then play that recording out loud while the FM-1 plays part #2 live on top. Two layers from one box.

Or put the free SLOOP firmware on it: then this same box becomes a 4-track groovebox (page ).
