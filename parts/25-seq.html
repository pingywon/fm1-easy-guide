Lesson 7

SEQ: record your first loop

The sequencer is a music box with 16 drawers. Put a note in each and it plays them in order, forever. There are 16 music boxes (patterns), and each remembers its own sound.

Why your sound changes when you press SEQ

Each pattern keeps a sticker saying which sound it plays, and pressing SEQ loads that sound. The Presets knob is switched off inside SEQ. So: save your sound first, then pick it inside SEQ (step 3).

  1. Get your sound ready. Press Save. Preset Saved
  2. ARP light off? Press SEQ. SEQ and STOP lights on
  3. Turn Select to page 2. Knob 1 Voice = your sound's number. Rate = 1/16, Tempo = 100. your sound is back
  4. Turn Select to page 1. Knob 1 Pattern = pick one. Knob 4 Step = 16.
  5. Press Rec. REC and PLAY lights on: it's listening
  6. Play notes one at a time. Each fills the next drawer. a bottom-key light for each filled drawer
  7. Press Rec again. your loop plays back. Play/Stop stops and starts it. Press Save to keep it.
  • Page 1: Pattern · Clear · Chain · Step (loop length).
  • Page 2: Voice (the sound) · Rate · Tempo · Gate.
  • Page 3: Swing · Sync · Transpose.
Lesson 7b

Fix a mistake, leave a gap

Everyone records a wrong note. Here's how to fix it, and how to put silence into a loop, which is what makes a beat groove.

I want to…Do this
Leave a silent stepWhile recording, hold Rec and turn Select one click. That drawer stays empty. Two clicks = two silent steps.
Fix one wrong noteWhile recording, hold Rec and turn Select to walk to that step. Let go of Rec and play the right note.
Erase a step or the whole patternPage 1, turn Knob 2 (Clear). Then Knob 3 = clear this step, Knob 4 = clear the whole pattern, Knob 2 = cancel.
Start again from the topPress Rec again. It records from step 1, writing over as it goes.
Make a shorter loopPage 1, Knob 4 Step = 8 (half as long) or 4.
Hold a note longerWhile recording, hold the key and turn Select to where it should end, then let go.
Watch out

Play more notes than the loop has steps and it wraps around to step 1 and writes over your first notes. Count as you go.
