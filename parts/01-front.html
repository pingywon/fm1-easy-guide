How to use this book

A synthesizer is a box that makes music sounds from electricity. The FM-1 is a small one with 128 ready-made sounds. This book teaches it one page at a time.

  • Do the lessons in order. Each is one page and takes 5–10 minutes. Tick the boxes as you go.
  • Keep the FM-1 in front of you. This is a do-it-with-me book.
  • Skip anything you don't need. Part 1 is for looking things up. Part 2 is the lessons. Part 3 is beats.

Why it's called "FM"

Picture 6 kids on swings. One kid swinging alone makes a smooth "oooo." When another kid pushes that swing, the sound turns shiny and bell-like. The harder the push, the buzzier it gets. The FM-1 builds every sound this way, exactly like the famous 1980s Yamaha DX7 keyboard. That's all the theory you need.

Before you start

  • Charge it with any USB-C cable, like a phone. A full charge lasts about 12 hours. The battery icon is top-right on the screen.
  • The power switch is on the back, next to the USB-C port.
  • Sound comes out of the built-in speaker. Plug headphones or an aux cable into the 3.5 mm jack on the back and the speaker turns itself off.

Your 60-second first go

  1. Flip the power switch. the screen lights up
  2. Turn Master to about half. The FM-1 is loud.
  3. Press any key. a sound, and a wiggly yellow line on the screen
  4. Turn Presets one click and press a key again. a different sound, and a new name at the top of the screen

That's it. You're playing a synthesizer. The rest of this book shows you how to change those sounds and record them.

Headphones

Use normal stereo headphones (the plug has 2 black rings). Phone headsets with 3 rings can buzz.

The 6 golden rules

Almost every "it's broken!" moment is one of these.

1. Buttons open drawers. FX ENV LFO ARP SEQ each open a drawer of settings. Press once to open, press the same button again to close.
2. The 4 knobs change jobs. Knob 1–Knob 4 do something different in every drawer. Their jobs show on the screen in a 2×2 grid: Knob 1 top-left, 2 top-right, 3 bottom-left, 4 bottom-right.
3. Select turns the page. Most drawers have 2 or 3 pages. The bottom of the screen shows which one, like 1/2.
4. Lost? Press Home. It closes whatever is open. The top line of the screen always shows the sound you're on.
5. Nothing is kept until you press Save. Hold it a moment, until the screen says Preset Saved.
6. ARP and SEQ can't both be on. Turn one off before you turn the other on.
