Part 1

The 128 sounds

The sounds come in 4 groups of 32. Groups A, B and C follow a simple pattern. Group D is the fun grab-bag: drums, bells and oddities.

  • Group A (001–032): PIANO, ORGAN, SYN LEAD, SYN PAD, repeating. So PIANO 1 is 001, PIANO 2 is 005, PIANO 3 is 009 … every 4th number. ORGAN starts at 002, SYN LEAD at 003, SYN PAD at 004.
  • Group B (033–064): GUITAR (033, 037 …), DS GUITAR = crunchy guitar (034 …), BASS (035 …), SYN BASS (036 …).
  • Group C (065–096): BRASS (065 …), WOODWIND (066 …), STRING (067 …), VOICE (068 …).

Good ones to start with

Calm: 004 SYN PAD 1, 118 KALIMBA. Beats: 127 PERCUSSION, 123 TOM TOMS. Bass: 035 BASS 1. Big: 065 BRASS 1.
