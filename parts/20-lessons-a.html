Lesson 1

First sounds

Goal: flip through the ready-made sounds and find three you like.

  1. Turn Master to half.
  2. Press the far-left bottom key, then the far-right one. left is low like a bear, right is high like a bird
  3. Press and hold one key for 3 seconds. some sounds keep going, some fade by themselves
  4. Press 3 keys at once. a chord: all three together
  5. Turn Presets one click. a list pops up for a second, and the sound changes right away
  6. Keep clicking and playing. There are 128 sounds; after 128 it goes back to 001.
  7. Found one you like? Write its number in the log on page . Find three.
Try it

The name at the top of the screen is a big hint: PIANO, ORGAN, BASS, BRASS, BELL… The full list is on page .

Lesson 2

Higher and lower

Goal: move the whole keyboard up and down, and know how to put it back.

  1. Play bottom key 1. Press Oct− once and play it again. the same note, one "floor" lower. The screen shows Octave -1
  2. Press Oct− twice more and play the low keys. deep rumbles, good for bass and drums
  3. Press Oct+ until the screen says Octave +3. tiny, bright notes, good for ticks and bells
  4. Press Oct− and Oct+ together. back to normal
Watch out

Holding an OCT button while turning Algorithm shifts every key by a tiny step (the screen says Transpose). It's remembered even after you switch off. If notes ever sound "off," press both OCT buttons together to reset.

Lesson 3

ENV: a sound's life story

Every sound is born, lives and goes to sleep. ENV lets you choose how, with 4 knobs.

  • Knob 1 Attack: how fast it wakes up. Low = instant, like a drum. High = a slow ghostly fade-in.
  • Knob 2 Decay: how fast it calms down after the first loud moment.
  • Knob 3 Sustain: how loud it stays while you hold the key.
  • Knob 4 Release: how long it takes to fade after you let go.
  1. Pick a sound. Press ENV. 4 bar graphs
  2. Turn Attack to a lot and hold a key. Then turn it to lowest. Hear the difference.
  3. Turn Release to a lot, tap a key, let go. Listen to the tail.
  4. Press ENV to close. Press Save if you like it.
Three recipes (Attack · Decay · Sustain · Release)

Plink: lowest · a little · lowest · a little.   Organ: lowest · half · highest · lowest.   Dreamy pad: a lot · half · a lot · a lot.

Lesson 4

LFO: the wobble hand

An LFO is an invisible hand that wiggles something for you, over and over. It can wobble the pitch (a siren) or the loudness (a helicopter).

  • Knob 1 Wave: the shape of the wiggle (pictures above).
  • Knob 2 Speed: how fast the hand wiggles.
  • Knob 3 PMD: how much it wiggles the pitch.
  • Knob 4 AMD: how much it wiggles the loudness.
  • Page 2, Knob 2 Pitch Sens: how much the pitch listens to the hand. Turn it up or PMD does nothing.
  1. Press LFO. Set Wave = Sine, Speed = 35, PMD = 10.
  2. Turn Select to page 2. Set Pitch Sens = 4. Hold a key. a singer's wobble
  3. Back on page 1, turn Speed up and PMD up. a police siren
  4. Set PMD and AMD back to 0 to switch the wobble off. Press LFO to close.
Lesson 5

FX: effects

Effects change a sound after it's made, like shouting in a canyon. The FM-1 has 6, in a row. A bright box on the screen means that effect is on.

  1. Press FX. a grid of 6 boxes with a blinking dot under one
  2. Turn Select to move the dot to Reverb.
  3. Turn Knob 4 to the right. the box goes bright: Reverb is on. Play a key: it's in a big room
  4. Turn Knob 3 (Mix) up and down while you play.
  5. Move the dot to Delay and turn it on with Knob 4. echo… echo… echo
  6. Press FX to close. Press Save if you like it.

Sound gone quiet?

A Filter with CutOff at lowest nearly silences everything. Turn the Filter off with Knob 4.

Lesson 6

ARP: it plays for you

Hold some keys and the arpeggiator plays them one after another, by itself, like a music box.

  1. Make sure the SEQ light is off. Press ARP. the ARP light comes on
  2. Hold bottom keys 1, 3 and 5 together. da-da-da, da-da-da, over and over
  3. Keep holding and turn Knob 4 (Tempo). faster or slower
  4. Turn Knob 1 (Mode) through the patterns below.
  5. Turn Select to page 2 and set Knob 3 Latch = On. Let go. it keeps playing without you
  6. To stop: Latch Off, or press ARP.
  • Modes: Up (low to high) · Down · Inclusive (bouncing) · Random · Order (the order you pressed) · Repeat (each note twice).
  • Page 1: Mode · Octave (how many floors it climbs) · Rate (1/16 = busy, 1/4 = slow) · Tempo.
  • Page 2: Gate (short "tik" or long) · Swing (50% straight, 60% bouncy) · Latch.
Screen says Disable SEQ to enter?

Press SEQ to turn the sequencer off first. The ARP is for playing live; it can't be recorded.
