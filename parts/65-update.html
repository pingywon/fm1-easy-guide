Software versions and updating

The FM-1 gets free software updates that add features. This book describes version V15 (July 2026). If a page doesn't match your screen, this is probably why.

Is mine on V15?

Press SEQ. If the bottom of the screen says 1/3 (three pages), you're on V15. Older software has two SEQ pages, no Voice setting, no Glide, and a factory reset that resets all 128 sounds at once.

What the printed manual gets wrong

The paper manual in the box is older than V15. Its SEQ and GLO pages are out of date, and it says ENV and FX go 0–8 when yours go 0–100. Trust the screen.

How to update (optional, needs a Windows or Mac computer)

  1. Charge the FM-1. Turn it on and connect it with a USB-C data cable.
  2. On m-vave.com, open the FM-1 product page. Download the M-UPGRADE app and the PC Firmware file (ends in .fwsc).
  3. Open M-UPGRADE and wait until it shows the FM-1 and its version.
  4. Click Select Firmware, choose the .fwsc file, then Update Firmware. The screen may go black for a moment.
  5. Wait for "Upgrade completed!", then unplug.
Don't

Don't unplug or switch off during the update. Don't click "Update Preset" unless you want some sounds replaced. If it says "Waiting for device reconnection," unplug and replug the cable and try again.
