Music words

The only music words this book uses, in plain English. Come back here whenever one trips you up.

NoteOne sound, like one "boop." Each key plays one note.
PitchHow high or low a note is. A bird is high, a bear is low. Keys to the right are higher.
OctaveThe same note one whole "floor" higher or lower, like a kid and a grown-up singing the same song.
PresetA saved recipe for a sound. The FM-1 has 128. The screen also calls it a voice. Same thing.
BeatThe steady tap you clap or tap your foot to.
TempoHow fast the beats go, in beats per minute (BPM). 60 = a clock ticking. 120 = a fast walk.
BarA group of 4 beats: "1-2-3-4."
RestSilence on purpose. Gaps are part of a beat.
LoopSomething that plays again and again.
ChordSeveral notes at the same time.
BassThe low notes everything else stands on.
Sequencer (SEQ)A music box with 16 boxes (steps) you fill with notes. One filled row is a pattern.
Arpeggiator (ARP)Hold some keys and it plays them one after another for you.
Envelope (ENV)A sound's life story: how it starts, stays and fades.
LFOAn invisible hand that wiggles the sound over and over.
Effect (FX)Something the sound goes through after it's made: an echo, a big room, crunch.
OperatorOne of the 6 "swing kids" inside every sound.
