Cheat sheet

Buttons

FXeffects. Select picks one, Knob 4 = on/off
ENVAttack · Decay · Sustain · Release
LFOwobble: Wave · Speed · PMD · AMD
Editbuild sounds; keys become OP1–6
GLOpage 2: reset · velocity · glide
Homeescape
Savekeep everything
ARPplays held keys for you
SEQ16-step music box
Oct−Oct+lower / higher; both = reset

Screen

Top line = your sound. Bottom line = the drawer and its page. Knob values sit in a 2×2 grid: 1 top-left, 2 top-right, 3 bottom-left, 4 bottom-right.

Record a loop

  1. Make the sound. Save.
  2. ARP off. Press SEQ.
  3. Page 2: Knob 1 Voice = your sound. Rate 1/16. Tempo.
  4. Page 1: Pattern. Step = 16.
  5. Rec. Play notes one by one. Gap = hold Rec + turn Select.
  6. Rec again to listen.
  7. Save.

Rescue

  1. Home. Turn off ARP / SEQ.
  2. Oct− + Oct+ together.
  3. Presets one click away and back.
  4. Power off and on.
Remember

Nothing is kept until you Save. ARP and SEQ can't both be on.
