Instant beat (no recording)

Hold a BOOM, a tick and a SNAP key in the right order, and the ARP drums for you.

  • Load MY DRUM. Press Oct− once. SEQ light off.
  • Press ARP. Page 1: Mode = Order · Octave = 1 · Rate = 1/8 · Tempo = 100.
  • Page 2: Gate = 30% · Swing = 50% · Latch = On.
  1. Hold bottom key 1 (BOOM) and keep holding.
  2. Add key 16 (tick), then 9 (SNAP), then 14 (tick). Use both hands.
  3. Let go of everything. "boom – tick – SNAP – tick," forever. Latch keeps it going.
  • Dance beat: hold only 1 then 16, Tempo 124.
  • Bouncy: page 2, Swing 62%.   Busier: page 1, Rate 1/16.   Wild: Mode Random.
  • Stop: Latch Off, or press ARP. To keep a beat, record it with the SEQ (next page).
