# FM-1 Easy Guide

A plain-words beginner's guide to the M-VAVE FM-1 pocket FM synthesizer — built because the official manual is genuinely hard to read, and it predates the V15 firmware that changed how the sequencer works.

The guide breaks the whole machine into one-page, follow-along lessons: every button in plain English, first sounds, your first recorded loop, and simple beats drawn as step grids. No music knowledge needed.

## Get it

- **Interactive follow-along demo** (click the panel, hear real sound): https://fm1-demo.pingywon.workers.dev
- **Project page**: https://pingywon.github.io/fm1-easy-guide/
- **SLOOP firmware tutorial** (for FM-1s running the free [SLOOP](https://github.com/isod89/sloop-fm1) groovebox firmware): https://fm1-demo.pingywon.workers.dev/sloop/
- **The guide (PDF, 33 pages, prints fine in black & white)**: [FM-1-Easy-Guide.pdf](FM-1-Easy-Guide.pdf)

## What's inside the PDF

- **Part 1 · Meet the box** — a numbered map of the panel, every control in one line, the music words, and all 128 factory sounds by name (the drums live in 099–127).
- **Part 2 · Ten lessons** — one page each: first sounds, octaves, ENV, LFO, effects, the arpeggiator, recording a loop, fixing mistakes, saving, building your own sound, plugging things in.
- **Part 3 · Beats** — how a beat works, turning a preset into a drum hit, then four beats step by step.
- **Help** — "it's doing something weird" fixes, firmware-version notes, a cheat sheet and a log page.

## The SLOOP tutorial

[SLOOP](https://github.com/isod89/sloop-fm1) by 3dSam (GPL-3.0, based on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita / Hügelton Instruments) is free replacement firmware that turns the FM-1 into a 4-track groovebox. The SLOOP tutorial (`demo/src/sloop.html`) teaches it on a clickable panel: six follow-along lessons (tracks, a step-recorded drum loop, live-recorded bass, one-finger chords, mixing and punch-in effects, song mode) and four ready-made grooves (lo-fi, boom-bap, house, acid) with build-it-yourself steps for the real device.

It is this project's own code: the sounds are lookalikes made with the browser's Web Audio, not SLOOP's engines, and nothing from the SLOOP repository is copied. Unofficial; not made by the SLOOP authors or M-VAVE.

## Build it yourself

Needs Python 3, Chromium and poppler-utils (`pdftotext`, `pdftoppm`).

```
python3 build.py              # renders FM-1-Easy-Guide.pdf from parts/*.html
python3 tools/build_site.py   # the project page in docs/
python3 tools/build_demo.py   # both demos into demo/site/ (bash demo/publish.sh deploy publishes them)
```

Pages are plain HTML in `parts/`, styled by `fm1.css`. Diagrams (the panel, keyboards, beat grids) are generated by macros in `build.py`, so they always match the text. The table of contents page numbers fill themselves in on a second render pass.

The current version lives in [`VERSION`](VERSION) and is stamped onto the cover, every page footer, the project page and the demo.

## Honesty notes

- Unofficial. Not made by or affiliated with M-VAVE. "M-VAVE", "FM-1", "DX7" and "Dexed" are names of their owners.
- Written against firmware **V15** (July 2026). Where sources disagreed with the printed manual, the guide says "trust the screen."
- The knob amounts are starting points worked out from the manual, M-VAVE's own update videos and owner forums — your ears are the judge.
